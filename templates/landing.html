Academic Governance

From classroom encoding to certified permanent records.

A disciplined four-stage lifecycle ensures that every official report card, Form 137, and institutional summary is backed by verified academic data.

Proceed to Sign In
  1. 01

    Stage One · Faculty

    Encode Academic Data

    Subject teachers and advisers record learner scores and quarterly grades within their assigned sections and grading periods.

  2. 02

    Stage Two · Registrar

    Review & Verify Submissions

    Submitted grade sheets are checked for completeness and accuracy; discrepancies can be formally returned with remarks for correction.

  3. 03

    Stage Three · Governance

    Validate & Lock Records

    Approved grades are officially validated and locked to safeguard record integrity prior to school-wide reporting and archiving.

  4. 04

    Stage Four · Certification

    Certify & Generate Reports

    Validated records immediately power official Report Cards, Form 137 (SF10) permanent records, SF6 summaries, and institutional analytics.
